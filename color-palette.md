Background `#1a1510` heartwood with:

- Body text: `#e8e0d4`
- Secondary text: `#8a8070`
- Buttons: jade-tinted — bg `rgba(74,124,101,0.15)`, border `rgba(74,124,101,0.35)`, text `#a8c8b8`

- Footer text: `#5a5040`
- Footer links: `#6a8070` → hover `#8aaa98`
- Footer border-top: `rgba(74,124,101,0.15)`
